    Orders fill at the last close. Every entry gets a stop just past the low (high, for a short) of the last 15 minutes, and at least one average 5-min bar away. The size is set so that hitting it loses the chosen share of your equity, within 4× your equity in total (intraday buying power). Drag the stop's line or label to move it, double-click it (double-tap on touch) to type a price, or press X to remove it. Pressing the opposite side reverses your position. Pressing your own side again adds the same risk again, measured to your current stop, and Trim ½ sells half. It fills at the stop, or at a bar's open if the bar gaps past it, and Play pauses there. When the chart runs out, any open trade is closed automatically; in 5-min mode that's the 16:00 close. Skip to move jumps ahead to the next bar with at least twice the usual range (or to 15:00), with your position riding along. Play speeds through quiet bars and slows down on big ones, unless Steady pace is on.

    Avg R is the average result in units of the risk you took at entry (entry to stop), so −1R is a full stop-out. Best / worst in the trade history is how far a trade went your way and against you while you held it (MFE / MAE). Profit factor is the dollars won over the dollars lost (for calls, the moves); above 1 makes money. Beat random replays your trades or calls on the same bars but with a coin-flip direction, 2,000 times, and shows the share of those runs your real direction beat. Around 50% is what luck looks like; 95% or more is hard to get by chance. Always long (or always up) is the average move if you had picked up every time. If it matches yours, the market's upward drift did the work. With several setups, one of them looking strong by luck alone is common, so trust a setup's number more once it has 50+ trades.
